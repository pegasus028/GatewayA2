## The pattern
**An island is land in water; a lake is water in land**

Two words, one idea turned inside out. An island is land with water all around it (Phuket, Ko Samui). A lake is water with land all around it (Songkhla Lake). Water words also differ in size and movement: a river moves (it flows to the sea), a lake stays still, and an ocean is enormous salt water (the Pacific, the Indian Ocean). A waterfall is a river that drops. Where land and water meet, you find a beach: sand or small stones next to the sea or a lake. Above everything is the sky. Fun test: a lake can have an island in it, and an island can have a lake on it!

Examples:
- There's a small **island** in the middle of the **lake**.
- The **river** goes all the way to the **ocean**.
- We walked along the **beach** and looked up at the **sky**.

Unit words that follow this pattern: island, lake, river, ocean, waterfall, beach, sky.
