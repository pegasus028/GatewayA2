## The pattern
**Silent letters and sneaky sounds**

Some letters on the page make no sound: island has no s (EYE-land), whale has no h (it sounds like 'wail'), and leopard has no o (LEP-ard). Other letters change their sound. The letters ow say /aʊ/ in owl (like cow) but /əʊ/ in snow (like go); flowers, cloud and mountain have the owl sound too. In wolf, the o sounds like the oo in good; in monkey, it sounds like the u in sun. Bear rhymes with chair, not with beer, and warm rhymes with storm, not farm. Three sounds need care: put your top teeth on your lip for /v/ in valley, river and wolves; finish owl, hill and waterfall with a real /l/; finish fox with /ks/.

Examples:
- An **owl** in the **snow**: same letters, different sounds!
- The **whale** swam around the **island**.
- Two **wolves** walked along the **valley**.

Unit words that follow this pattern: island, whale, leopard, owl, snow, flowers, cloud, mountain, wolf, monkey, bear, warm, storm, valley, river, hill, waterfall, fox.

## Try it (end the episode with this)
Read the three example sentences aloud slowly, then fast. Record yourself and listen for /v/, /l/ and /aʊ/.
