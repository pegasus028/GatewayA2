## The pattern
**Every animal has an address — and some addresses are disappearing**

Match each animal to its home and you remember two words at once. The ocean: whale, shark, jellyfish, penguin. Rivers and lakes: hippo. The forest: monkey, tiger, leopard, owl, bear. Fields and flowers: bee, butterfly. Mountains and the sky: eagle. Warm, dry, rocky places: lizard, scorpion, snake. Forests and cold, snowy places: wolf, fox. Rats live wherever people live! When people destroy a forest or a river, the animals lose their address. That is one big reason why some species are now rare, and why the news about them is often shocking. When the last male northern white rhino died in 2018, only two were left, and both were female.

Examples:
- **Whales** live in the **ocean**, and **hippos** live in **rivers** and **lakes**.
- **Bees** visit the **flowers** in the **field**.
- If we **destroy** the **forest**, the tigers will lose their home.

Unit words that follow this pattern: whale, shark, jellyfish, penguin, hippo, monkey, tiger, leopard, owl, bear, bee, butterfly, eagle, lizard, scorpion, snake, wolf, fox, rat, rhino, ocean, river, lake, forest, field, flowers, mountain, sky, snowy, destroy, species, rare, shocking.

## Try it (end the episode with this)
Pick three animals. For each one, say 'A ___ lives in/on ___.' Then name one thing that could destroy that home.
