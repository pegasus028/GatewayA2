## The pattern
**heavy rain, strong wind, thick fog**

Some words are partners: English speakers always put them together, so learn them as pairs. Rain is heavy (or light): heavy rain. Snow is heavy too: heavy snow. Wind is strong (or light): a strong wind, strong winds. Fog is thick: thick fog. Storms are big or bad: a big storm. If you translate word by word you may say strong rain or big wind. People will understand you, but it doesn't sound natural. A picture helps: rain and snow fall down on you, so they are heavy; wind pushes you, so it is strong; fog is like a blanket you can't see through, so it is thick.

Examples:
- There was **heavy rain** last night.
- A **strong wind** blew my hat into the river.
- We couldn't see the road in the **thick fog**.

Unit words that follow this pattern: rain, snow, wind, fog, storm.

## Try it (end the episode with this)
