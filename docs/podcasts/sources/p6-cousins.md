## The pattern
**Food families: cousins on your plate**

Some foods are related. From milk: cream is the thick fat at the top of milk; beat cream for a long time and you get butter; add good bacteria to milk and you get yoghurt; mix milk with ice cream and you get a milkshake. These are dairy. Eggs are often sold next to the milk, but they come from birds, so eggs are not dairy. Plant cousins: onions and garlic are in the same family; cucumbers and melons are in the same family; beans, lentils and peanuts are all in the bean family; and broccoli and cabbage are the same plant species, changed by farmers over many years. Learn words in families and they stick together.

Examples:
- milk → cream → butter
- milk + good bacteria → yoghurt
- onion and garlic: the same family
- broccoli and cabbage: the same species

Unit words that follow this pattern: cream, butter, yoghurt, milkshake, egg, onion, garlic, cucumber, melon, bean, lentil, nut, broccoli, cabbage.
