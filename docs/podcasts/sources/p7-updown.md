## The pattern
**From rice field to mountain top: the land has levels**

Imagine walking from a rice field to the top of Doi Inthanon. You start on flat land: a field, with grass or rice. The land starts to go up: a hill, higher than a field but lower than a mountain, usually round and green. Keep climbing: a mountain, very high, with rocks and cool air at the top. Now look down between two hills or mountains: that low land is a valley, and because water always runs to the lowest place, a valley often has a river. When a river drops over high rocks, you get a waterfall. Everything growing on this land is plants: grass, flowers and trees (lots of trees = a forest).

Examples:
- The **field** is flat, the **hill** is a bit higher and the **mountain** is very high.
- A **river** runs along the **valley**.
- **Grass** and **flowers** are both **plants**.

Unit words that follow this pattern: field, hill, mountain, valley, river, waterfall, grass, flowers, plants, forest.
