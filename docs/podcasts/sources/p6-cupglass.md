## The pattern
**Hot in a cup, cold in a glass**

A cup has a handle, so you can hold a hot drink without burning your fingers: a cup of tea. A glass has no handle and you can see the drink inside, so we use it for cold drinks: a glass of water, a glass of milkshake, a glass of lemonade or orange juice. (Thai iced tea is cold, so it comes in a glass!) The word glass also follows the 'stuff or thing?' pattern: glass (uncountable) is the hard, clear material; a glass (countable) is the thing you drink from. So you can have a glass of water in a glass made of glass. Bottles and jars are often made of glass too.

Examples:
- Grandpa drinks a cup of hot tea.
- Mint drinks a glass of cold milkshake.
- This jar is made of glass.
- Two glasses of water, please.

Unit words that follow this pattern: cup, glass, tea, water, milkshake, lemonade, orange juice, bottle, jar.
