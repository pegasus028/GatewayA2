## The pattern
**Little words inside big words**

Many food words are two small words stuck together: milk + shake = milkshake, pan + cake = pancake, straw + berry = strawberry, take + away = takeaway. These are written as one word. Some pairs stay as two words: orange juice, soft drink (soft here means 'with no alcohol', not soft to touch!). Lemonade is lemon + -ade, an ending for fruit drinks. Other words hide old stories: hamburger comes from the city of Hamburg (burger is the short form); biscuit comes from old French for 'cooked twice'; salad and sausage both come from 'sal', the Latin word for salt. Knowing the parts helps you remember the meaning and the spelling.

Examples:
- milk + shake → milkshake
- pan + cake → pancake
- take + away → takeaway
- lemon + -ade → lemonade

Unit words that follow this pattern: milkshake, pancake, strawberry, takeaway, orange juice, soft drink, lemonade, burger, biscuit, salad, sausage, salt.
