## The pattern
**cold → warm → hot, and wet ↔ dry**

Temperature words sit on one line: cold → (cool) → warm → hot. Warm is pleasant, like a December afternoon in Chiang Mai. Hot is strong, like Bangkok in April. Wet and dry are a different line: they tell you about water, not heat. A wet day, wet hair, the wet season; a dry towel, the dry season. Thailand's year sits on both lines: the hot season (about March to May), the rainy season (about May to October) and the cool, dry season (about November to February). Careful: rainy describes only weather, but wet describes weather and things. ✗ My shirt is rainy → ✓ My shirt is wet.

Examples:
- It's **cold** on Doi Inthanon, **warm** in Chiang Mai and **hot** in Bangkok.
- My clothes are **wet**. Can I **dry** them in the sun?
- The cool season is usually **dry**.

Unit words that follow this pattern: cold, warm, hot, wet, dry, rainy, sunny, icy.
