## The pattern
**in a forest, on a beach: the little word before the place**

Ask one question: am I inside a space, or on top of a surface? A space is all around you: in a forest, in a field, in a valley, in the sky, in the ocean, in a river or lake (when you swim). A surface is under your feet: on a beach, on an island, on a hill, on a mountain, on the grass, and on a river or lake (in a boat). One pair to remember: in the mountains (a whole mountain area: We went skiing in the mountains) but on the mountain (on its side or top). At is also fine for a place you visit: at the beach. The most common mistake? On the sky. The sky is a space, so it's in.

Examples:
- Monkeys live **in** the forest, and eagles fly **in** the sky.
- We camped **on** an island and played football **on** the beach.
- Hippos spend the day **in** the river.

Unit words that follow this pattern: forest, field, valley, sky, ocean, river, lake, beach, island, hill, mountain, grass.
