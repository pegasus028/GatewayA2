## The pattern
**A chicken runs; some chicken is on your plate**

Some food words name a whole thing AND the food we cut from it. The whole animal is countable: a chicken, two fish (fish keeps the same plural). The meat is uncountable: some chicken, some fish. Big food that we cut up works the same way: a melon, a cabbage, a lettuce, a pizza (the whole one) → some melon, some pizza (a part). Dishes and drinks too: a curry, a salad, two teas when you order → some curry, some tea. Pepper has two meanings: a pepper (the vegetable), some pepper (the black powder). Burgers and sausages are meat in a shape, so they are always countable. In Unit 7 you will meet wild animals — all countable: a tiger, two whales.

Examples:
- There's a chicken in the garden. / I had some chicken for lunch.
- Leo caught three fish. / We had fish for dinner.
- We ordered a pizza. / Is there any pizza left?
- Two teas, please! / Would you like some tea?

Unit words that follow this pattern: chicken, fish, melon, cabbage, lettuce, pizza, curry, salad, tea, pepper, burger, sausage.
