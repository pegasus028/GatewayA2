## The pattern
**Same snack, different country**

Your book uses British English, but films and YouTube often use American English. The food doesn't change — only the word does. UK crisps = US chips (and UK chips are hot potato sticks, US French fries). A UK packet of crisps is a US bag of chips. UK biscuits = US cookies (a US biscuit is a soft bread roll!). UK sweets = US candy. UK fizzy drink or soft drink = US soda. UK a tin of beans = US a can of beans (in the UK, drinks always come in cans). UK takeaway = US takeout. Spelling changes too: yoghurt (UK), yogurt (US). And British lemonade is usually fizzy; American lemonade usually isn't. Use the British words in tests — but understand both.

Examples:
- UK: a packet of crisps — US: a bag of chips
- UK: a biscuit — US: a cookie
- UK: sweets — US: candy
- UK: a takeaway — US: takeout

Unit words that follow this pattern: crisps, packet, biscuit, sweets, soft drink, tin, can, takeaway, yoghurt, lemonade.
