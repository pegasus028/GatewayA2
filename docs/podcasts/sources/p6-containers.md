## The pattern
**The food chooses its container**

Two questions pick the container. First: what is the food like? Liquid → bottle, carton or can (and a cup or a glass when you drink it). Thick and sticky → jar. Dry pieces → packet, bag or box. Cooked and kept for months → tin (or can). Second: what is the container made of? Glass → bottle, jar, glass. Card (thick paper) → carton, box. Metal → can, tin. Paper or plastic → bag, packet. Put the two answers together: liquid + card = a carton of orange juice; sticky + glass = a jar of honey; fizzy + metal = a can of soft drink; dry pieces + paper = a packet of biscuits; cooked + metal = a tin of beans.

Examples:
- a carton of orange juice — liquid + card
- a jar of honey — sticky + glass
- a can of soft drink — fizzy + metal
- a packet of biscuits — dry pieces + paper

Unit words that follow this pattern: bag, bottle, box, can, carton, cup, glass, jar, packet, tin, water, orange juice, soft drink, honey, jam, crisps, biscuit, sweets, rice, sugar, bean, soup.

## Try it (end the episode with this)
Walk round a 7-Eleven in your head. Find one thing for each container: bottle, carton, can, jar, packet, bag, box, tin.
