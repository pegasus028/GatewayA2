# Storybook Art Brief — Trail Mix (Gateway A2 Units 6 & 7)

This is the only source for the illustration notebook. It describes ONE illustration per slide. Every slide is a full-bleed picture with no words on it.

## Style for every image
Warm watercolour-and-ink children’s storybook illustration, soft natural light, delicate ink outlines, realistic modern Thai and world settings. No text, letters, numbers, logos, brand names or writing anywhere in the picture (not on signs, packets, screens or clothes).

## The two characters (identical in every image)
- **Tonkla**: 13-year-old Thai boy, short spiky black hair, round cheerful face, plain red T-shirt (a puffy red jacket in the snowy chapter).
- **Fah**: 13-year-old Thai girl, chin-length straight black hair with a straight fringe, plain mustard-yellow T-shirt (a dark green jacket in the snowy chapter), a small black camera on a green strap round her neck in every image.
